CLASS A — instrument wording rights.

When you email, remember to:
1. cc itemresponsewarehouse@stanford.edu and bdomingue@stanford.edu
2. insert the instrument name, the item count, and links at the end
3. name the specific version/edition -- rights often differ between the
   original and a short form or a translation
4. log the send in processing_notes/outreach/outreach_log.csv

WHAT THIS ASK IS, AND IS NOT. The ask is permission to redistribute the item
wording alongside response data that is already openly licensed. It is not a
licence to administer the instrument, not a request for scoring keys or norms,
and not commercial use. Say so explicitly -- most refusals we have on record
read as though the writer assumed a broader request.

###email text below
Hello,

My name is Ben Domingue. I am an associate professor in the Graduate School of
Education at Stanford University. I maintain the Item Response Warehouse (IRW),
a public repository of item response datasets used for psychometric and
methodological research: https://itemresponsewarehouse.org

The IRW holds response data from studies that administered the [INSTRUMENT].
That response data is already public under an open licence from the original
investigators. What the IRW cannot currently show is the text of the items
themselves, so a user can see that a respondent answered 3 to item 7 without
being able to see what item 7 asked. For methodological work -- differential
item functioning, item difficulty modelling, cross-instrument comparison --
that wording is often what makes the data usable.

I am writing to ask whether you would permit the IRW to publish the [N] item
stems and response options of the [INSTRUMENT] alongside that response data,
with attribution to you and a citation to the original publication.

To be clear about the scope of what I am asking:

- This is not a request to license the instrument for administration. Anyone
  wanting to use the [INSTRUMENT] in their own study would still come to you.
- It covers the item wording and response anchors only -- not scoring keys,
  norms, manuals, or interpretive material.
- It is limited to the specific version named above.
- The IRW is non-commercial and freely accessible.
- I would attribute the instrument to you on the record for every dataset that
  uses it, in a form you are welcome to specify.

If it would help, I am glad to add a note recording any conditions you would
like attached -- for example that the wording is pinned to one version, that
derivative translations are not licensed, or specific citation language. If you
would rather not have the wording redistributed, that is an entirely
satisfactory answer and I will record it so that neither I nor anyone working
on the IRW asks you again.

I am happy to discuss any questions or concerns.

Thank you for your consideration,
Ben

Instrument. [name, version, item count]
Example dataset in the IRW. [link]
IRW data standard. https://itemresponsewarehouse.org/standard.html
